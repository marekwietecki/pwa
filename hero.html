    How does HabitBubbl work?

    1 Rodzaje aktywności

    ✅

    Task

    One-time action planned for specific day. Do it, tick it off and forget!

    🔥

    Habit

    Repeatable activity, which You continuously complete. Keep the streak!

    🎯

    Goal

    Long-term goal - specify it and strive for acheivement.

    2 Task management

    ➕

    Adding: Tap the plus on the right bottom of the screen. Choose the category, enter the name and other details.

    ✏️

    Edit: Przytrzymaj dłużej kartę zadania, aby zmienić szczegóły.

    🗑️

    Delete: Tap the three dots and then tap the bin.

    3 Wybór Nawyków
